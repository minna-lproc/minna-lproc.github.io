Source of the Community List

The province-to-community mapping is based on the “IP groups per Province with Ancestral Domains/Ancestral Lands” list supplied through correspondence from NCIP Region XI. Community names are preserved as provided.

Important Mapping Limitation

This page visualizes province-level associations only. It does not reproduce CADT, CALT, ancestral domain, ancestral land, barangay, municipal, or community boundary data. Representative marker coordinates are used only for navigation and exploration.
