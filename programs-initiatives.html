Research translated into meaningful action

MinNa LProc R&D Lab advances language documentation, natural language processing, and responsible artificial intelligence for Philippine and Mindanao languages. Our work connects research, community engagement, technology development, and capacity building.

Research Programs

Sustained programs that guide the laboratory’s work

These programs establish the long-term direction of MinNa’s research, community engagement, technology development, and researcher formation.

Program

Indigenous Language Documentation and Digital Preservation

Supports the responsible collection, digitization, organization, validation, and preservation of language resources in collaboration with language communities.

  • Community-informed documentation and language resource collection
  • Digitization and structured storage of text, lexical, and related language data
  • Resource validation, attribution, and responsible dissemination
Communities and research partners Explore communities
Program

Language Technology Research and Development

Develops natural language processing tools, language technologies, and digital resources for Philippine languages, particularly low-resource and underrepresented languages.

  • Machine translation and multilingual language processing
  • Dialogue systems, text processing, and language resource tools
  • Model evaluation, linguistic analysis, and prototype validation
Researchers and technology teams View related projects
Program

Indigenous Languages in AI and Language Models

Investigates how language models and artificial intelligence systems can better represent, support, and responsibly include Indigenous and low-resource languages.

  • Language model experimentation, adaptation, and benchmarking
  • Corpus development for low-resource language inclusion
  • Responsible AI practices for culturally and linguistically grounded systems

Program

Research Capacity Building, Mentorship, and Training

Strengthens the local research community by supporting students, faculty members, and partner institutions in NLP, language technology, and responsible AI.

  • Research mentoring and technical guidance
  • Workshops, seminars, and applied learning activities
  • Collaborative research formation and publication support

Laboratory Services

Research and technical support for meaningful language technology work

MinNa works with students, researchers, institutions, government agencies, and community partners on projects aligned with the laboratory’s expertise and mandate.

NLP and AI Research Consultation

Technical consultation on research problems, study design, language technology methods, model selection, evaluation, and responsible AI considerations.

Research Design Technical Review
02

Corpus, Dataset, and Language Resource Development

Guidance and collaborative support for collecting, cleaning, organizing, annotating, validating, documenting, and preparing language datasets.

Corpus Building Annotation Data Documentation

NLP Prototype and Language Technology Development

Collaborative design and development of research prototypes such as translation tools, text-processing systems, dialogue systems, and language resource applications.

Prototyping Evaluation Deployment Guidance
04

Workshops, Seminars, and Capacity-Building Activities

Customized learning activities on NLP, machine translation, language models, AI agents, research methods, and responsible development.

Training Hands-on Workshops
