Why MinNa Matters

Preserving language while expanding participation in AI

MinNa LProc R&D Lab develops advanced Natural Language Processing tools, technologies, digital language resources, and AI-driven solutions for endangered, underrepresented, and low-resource Philippine languages.

Its work combines language documentation, resource development, machine translation, dialogue systems, language-model research, researcher training, and strategic policy development. The goal is not only to preserve language—but to ensure that communities are meaningfully represented in the digital and AI era.

Create lasting language resources Collect, digitize, curate, and share linguistic data for research and preservation.
Advance language AI Study models and architectures for multilingual and low-resource contexts.
Grow Mindanao-based expertise Train researchers, students, developers, and interdisciplinary collaborators.
Shape responsible innovation Support roadmaps, policies, and community-centered technology development.
Institutional Support

Strengthened by research investment and institutional partnership

MinNa was established with funding support from DOST-PCIEERD , including the provision of high-end research equipment essential to the laboratory’s work. The University of the Immaculate Conception serves as the implementing institution, providing the academic, administrative, and operational support needed to advance MinNa’s research, development, implementation, and technology deployment initiatives.
