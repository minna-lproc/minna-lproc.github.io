Our Impact Pathway

MinNa’s key performance indicators are organized around four interconnected action areas: Document, Digitize, Revitalize, and Empower. Together, they show how language knowledge becomes accessible resources, practical technologies, stronger research capacity, and more meaningful participation in the digital and AI ecosystem.

View the Pathway View Sample Dashboard
Four Connected Actions

The MinNa Impact Pathway

Each stage builds on the previous one: languages and knowledge are documented, transformed into accessible digital resources, used to support language vitality and technology, and returned as opportunities for communities, researchers, and institutions.

Step 01

Document

Record language, oral knowledge, cultural expressions, and community perspectives through ethical and collaborative research.

Step 02

Digitize

Convert validated materials into structured corpora, lexicons, datasets, repositories, and reusable digital language resources.

Step 03

Revitalize

Use research, education, NLP tools, language models, and digital platforms to strengthen language visibility, use, and continuity.

Step 04

Empower

Build the capacity of communities, students, researchers, and partner institutions to create, govern, and sustain language technologies.

Intended impact Philippine and Mindanao languages become better preserved, more accessible, more visible in research and technology, and more meaningfully represented in the digital and AI ecosystem.

Key Performance Indicators

Document

Capture language and cultural knowledge
  • Communities and languages engagedNumber of language communities and varieties included in documentation activities.
  • Materials collectedRecordings, interviews, texts, word lists, translations, and cultural narratives gathered.
  • Materials validatedResources reviewed with community contributors and language experts.
  • Community participationSpeakers, leaders, contributors, and local partners involved.

Digitize

Transform materials into reusable resources
  • Resources digitizedPhysical or raw materials converted into accessible digital formats.
  • Datasets and corpora developedStructured corpora, lexicons, parallel datasets, annotations, and metadata records.
  • Data quality and completenessResources meeting documentation, annotation, metadata, and validation standards.
  • Resources made accessibleRepositories, tools, collections, or controlled-access resources released.

Revitalize

Support use, visibility, and continuity
  • Language technologies developedNLP models, translation systems, dialogue systems, tools, and prototypes created.
  • Technologies evaluatedModel performance, user feedback, community review, and validation results.
  • Language use supportedEducational, research, community, or digital applications using the resources.
  • Adoption and sustained useUsage, deployment, reuse, downloads, or continued integration of tools.

Empower

Strengthen people, partnerships, and ownership
  • People trained and mentoredStudents, faculty, researchers, developers, and community participants trained.
  • Research participationStudent, faculty, and community-led studies, theses, capstones, or collaborative projects.
  • Partnerships strengthenedActive institutional, government, academic, and community collaborations.
  • Knowledge and policy outputsPublications, presentations, policy recommendations, roadmaps, and sustained initiatives.

This pathway connects MinNa’s research activities with the long-term goal of building a more linguistically inclusive digital future.
