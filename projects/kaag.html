About the project

Preserving voices. Celebrating identities.

KAAG—A Mobile-based Multimodal Preservation of Language and Culture—explores how digital technology can support the documentation, appreciation, and continued visibility of Indigenous languages and cultural traditions.

LanguageSupporting the visibility of underrepresented linguistic heritage.
CultureCelebrating the traditions and knowledge that shape community identities.
TechnologyUsing accessible digital experiences to connect people with heritage.
Project focus

Culture belongs in the digital future.

KAAG brings together three complementary priorities in the preservation of language and culture.

🗣️

Language preservation

Encouraging awareness and appreciation of Indigenous languages, especially those with limited representation in digital spaces.

🪡

Cultural documentation

Recognizing the importance of preserving cultural expressions, traditions, and community knowledge for future generations.

📱

Mobile accessibility

Bringing heritage-focused digital resources closer to learners, researchers, communities, and anyone interested in cultural diversity.
