The Manobo Languages

A language group with diverse dialects in Mindanao

The Manobo language is presented on the original page as a group of languages spoken in the Philippines. It is described as part of the Austronesian language family and as being spoken by Manobo communities primarily located in Mindanao.

It is also noted that the Manobo language group is divided into several dialects, each with its own characteristics and features.

Pagsalbar tu nigkainuy inuy kawa nu inikagian, isa nu inikagian tu kada katigayunan.“Saving endangered languages, one language at a time.”
