Who We Are

Language technology rooted in Mindanao

MinNa LProc R&D Lab is a research and development laboratory dedicated to advancing Natural Language Processing, digital language resources, and responsible AI for Philippine and indigenous languages.

The laboratory brings together researchers, developers, language experts, students, communities, and institutional partners to create technologies that preserve linguistic knowledge and make digital services more inclusive.

Build meaningful language resources Corpora, lexicons, translations, annotations, and recordings for research and preservation.
Advance inclusive language AI Machine translation, language models, speech technologies, and dialogue systems for low-resource contexts.
Co-create with communities Research and innovation grounded in cultural respect, community benefit, and responsible data practices.
Our Vision
A digital future where every language—regardless of its status—thrives, is represented, and is meaningfully included in AI and the broader digital ecosystem.
Our Mission

Research, innovation, and community engagement

MinNa advances the documentation, preservation, revitalization, and technological inclusion of Philippine and indigenous languages through research, development, and community-engaged innovation in NLP and Artificial Intelligence.

Language Documentation and Digital Resource Creation

Collect, document, digitize, curate, and steward linguistic resources for Philippine and indigenous languages.

Learn more

MinNa supports the creation of corpora, lexicons, annotations, translations, recordings, and other digital language assets for research, education, preservation, and computational modeling. This work emphasizes ethical data practices, community participation, and long-term accessibility.

NLP Research and AI for Indigenous Languages

Study language models, machine translation, speech technologies, and dialogue systems for low-resource contexts.

Learn more

MinNa examines how indigenous and low-resource Philippine languages can be more meaningfully represented and supported in emerging AI systems. This includes multilingual NLP, morphology-aware modeling, evaluation, and responsible AI development.

Inclusive Language Technologies for Real-World Use

Design, build, evaluate, and deploy NLP applications that respond to practical community and institutional needs.

Learn more

The laboratory develops language technologies and AI-enabled applications for education, health communication, cultural documentation, governance, and community development, helping make information and services more accessible across languages.

Research Capacity Building in Mindanao

Train and mentor emerging NLP researchers, developers, students, and collaborators in responsible language technology R&D.

Learn more

Through mentoring, collaborative projects, workshops, publications, and interdisciplinary partnerships, MinNa strengthens Mindanao-based expertise in NLP, AI, and digital language resource development.

Partnerships, Policy, and Responsible Innovation

Collaborate with communities, government, academe, and industry to guide ethical and inclusive language AI development.

Learn more

MinNa works with indigenous communities, local government units, schools, health institutions, research organizations, and technology partners to co-create language technologies, inform policy recommendations, and advance responsible innovation.
